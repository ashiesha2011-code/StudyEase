StudyEase is an AI-powered study platform built specifically for CBSE students. It helps you understand concepts, track your progress, practise with quizzes, and manage exam stress through mindfulness tools.

We're a student-built platform and we're constantly improving. By using StudyEase, you agree to these terms. We've kept them as short and human as possible — no legal jargon.
