Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  Questions? support@studyease.in

👋 The short version

We collect only what we need to make StudyEase work well for you. We never sell your data. We never share it with advertisers or third parties. You can delete your account and all your data any time by emailing us.

✅ Your data is yours. We're a study platform, not an advertising business. The only reason we store anything is to give you a better learning experience.

📋 What data we collect

Here's everything we store about you — no hidden surprises:

Data Why we collect it
Email address To create and access your account
Name To personalise your dashboard greeting and profile
Class To show you relevant content for your grade
Profile photo Optional — to show in your topbar and sidebar. Stored as a compressed image in our database.
Quiz scores To show you your progress over time in the Progress page
Session scores To track how you're doing across live study sessions
Mood check-ins To help you reflect on your wellbeing and show you trends in the Progress page
Breathing & meditation sessions To log mindfulness activity (type, duration, mode) and show it in progress tracking
AI chat history To show you past conversations in the AI Companion sidebar and maintain context within a session

🎯 How we use your data

  • To personalise your dashboard — your name, class, and avatar
  • To show your progress — quiz scores, session history, and mindfulness activity
  • To power the AI Companion — your messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI model to generate responses. We pass recent conversation history for context.
  • To remember your preferences — font size, mood check-in state, and session counts (stored locally in your browser)
  • To improve StudyEase — we may look at anonymised usage patterns to understand what features are working

🚫 What we never do

🔒 We never sell your data. We never share your personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or third parties for commercial purposes. Ever.

  • We don't show you ads based on your data
  • We don't share your chat history or mood check-ins with anyone
  • We don't build profiles of you to sell to other companies
  • We don't use your data to train AI models (Anthropic's usage policies apply separately to API calls)

🗄️ Where your data is stored

All your data is stored securely on Supabase, a hosted database platform with industry-standard encryption. Every table in our database has Row Level Security (RLS) enabled — this means your data is protected at the database level so only you can read or modify your own records, even if there were a bug in our code.

Supabase stores data in data centres that comply with modern security and privacy standards. You can read Supabase's own privacy policy at supabase.com/privacy.

AI messages are processed by Anthropic's API. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to that processing — see anthropic.com/privacy.

🍪 Cookies and local storage

We don't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. Here's what we do use:

  • Supabase auth session — stored in your browser's local storage to keep you logged in. This is just your login token, not personal data.
  • Preferences — font size setting (se_fontsize), mood check-in state (se_mood), and mindfulness session count (se_breath) are saved in localStorage. This data never leaves your device.
  • Session storage — used to control the dashboard splash screen so it only shows once per browser session.

You can clear all of this by clearing your browser's storage for this site. It won't delete your account or database records.

🔑 Your rights

You're in control of your data.

  • Access — your profile, scores, and chat history are visible directly in the app
  • Edit — update your name, class, and photo any time in Settings
  • Delete — you can delete your account from Settings → Danger zone. This permanently removes your profile, scores, chat history, and all other data from our database. It cannot be undone.
  • Request a copy — email us at support@studyease.in and we'll send you an export of your data

👦 Children's privacy

StudyEase is designed for students aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has created an account, please contact us at support@studyease.in and we will delete the account promptly.

For students aged 13–18, we encourage parents to review this policy. We've designed the platform to be safe and appropriate for younger students — there are no ads, no social features, and no sharing of personal information.

🔄 Changes to this policy

If we make any significant changes to this privacy policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top and do our best to notify active users via email. Continued use of StudyEase after changes means you accept the updated policy.

We'll never make changes that reduce your privacy rights without giving you notice first.
